# DoseMate TV Pitch

## Value Proposition

DoseMate TV helps families manage daily medicine routines together using the Apple TV as a shared health dashboard.

## Problem

Medicine reminders are usually private phone alarms. In a family, this creates a problem because one person may forget, miss the alarm, or fail to communicate with others.

## Solution

DoseMate TV shows the whole household who has taken medicine, what is due next, and which doses are urgent.

## Target Market

- Families with elderly members
- Caregivers
- Busy households
- Shared apartments
- Students managing regular medicine

## Revenue Model

- Free basic dashboard
- Premium family sync
- Caregiver notifications
- Refill tracking
- Pharmacy partnership promotions

## Go-To-Market Strategy

1. Launch the iOS DoseMate app first.
2. Add tvOS as a companion product.
3. Promote it as a family care and elder-care solution.
4. Partner with pharmacies and healthcare awareness campaigns.

## Market Potential

Families increasingly use digital tools for health routines. A TV-based shared medicine dashboard is simple, visible, and easy for older users to understand.

## Demo Script

1. Open DoseMate TV.
2. Explain the family cards.
3. Select each family member.
4. Show the medicine schedule.
5. Point out urgent dose highlighting.
6. Open the Pitch screen.
7. Explain the business value.
